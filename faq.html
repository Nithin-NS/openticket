Yes, OpenTickets provides free and secure one-click pass transfers. Simply open your order in the My Tickets section, click "Transfer Pass", and enter the recipient's mobile number or email address.

Once the recipient accepts, the original QR code is instantly revoked and a brand new dynamic token is minted directly to their account, preventing unauthorized double entry.

We accept all major South African payment options with zero hidden checkout fees:

  • Ozow Instant EFT: Instant bank authorization with Standard Bank, FNB, ABSA, Nedbank, Investec, and Discovery Bank.
  • Capitec Pay: 1-click in-app approval on your mobile for seamless authentication.
  • Visa & Mastercard: Local and international credit and debit cards protected by mandatory 3D-Secure 2.0 OTP verification.

All prices are quoted in South African Rand (ZAR) and are fully inclusive of 15% VAT.

If an event is cancelled by the event promoter or organizer, you will receive an automatic 100% refund credited back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

For rescheduled dates, your existing passes remain automatically valid for the replacement date. If you cannot attend the new fixture or date, you can request a full refund via our one-click claim button within 14 days of the rescheduling announcement.
